# Position Hierarchy

## ADMINISTRATION

**GM Office**

* Vice President

**Finance \& Accounting**

* Finance \& Accounting Head
* Accounting Assistant
* Cashier Head

**Management**

* Senior Duty Officer
* Duty Officer

**Human Resources**

* HR Head
* HR Assistant
* Company Nurse/HR Staff

**Sales \& Marketing**

* Sales \& Marketing Head
* Marketing/Reservation Clerk
* Graphic Artist

**Food \& Beverage**

* Food \& Beverage Coordinator

**Information Technology**

* System \& Network Officer
* Intern

**Logistics**

* Stockman

\---

## OPERATIONS

**Front Office**

* Front Desk Attendant
* Front Desk Butler
* Telecom Operator/Cashier

**Food \& Beverage**

* Waiter

**Kitchen**

* Assistant Chief Cook
* Cook
* Kitchen Helper

**Housekeeping**

* Leadman
* Room Attendant

**Maintenance**

* Maintenance Head
* Maintenance Man
* Maintenance Assistant
* Aircon Technician

**Services**

* Company Driver

**Security**

* Security Guard

\---

> \*\*Notes\*\*
> - Some employees may hold two positions due to the nature of their work.
> - Intern appears as both a position and an employment type; they are treated as separate fields in the system.
> - Logistics (Stockman) is placed under Administration as the role is cross-departmental and not limited to a single operations area.
> - Front Office replaces "Front Desk" as the standard sub-department name per hotel industry convention.
